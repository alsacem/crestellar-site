Advertising and Your Choices

From version 4.7 of the app, guests and free accounts see ads. The ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Every paid plan (Plus, Pro, Max 5× and Max 20×) shows no ads.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects your approximate location (estimated from your IP address), device identifiers, information about the ads you see and tap and about how you use the app, and diagnostic, performance and crash data. Google uses this information to show ads, to measure them and for analytics.

The advertising identifier (IDFA) is read only if you allow tracking when Apple asks. A signed-in free account is asked once, after its first finished quiz, after a short explanation from the app with a single button. You can change your answer at any time in your device's Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later in the app, in Setting ▸ Privacy choices.

When you watch an ad to earn tokens, the app sends Google your account's internal ID (never your name or phone number) and a random code, so that our server can confirm the reward with Google. Each credit is recorded.

To learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Some ads may come from Pangle, an ad network that competes through Google AdMob for the ads in the app; its SDK is part of the app. Pangle's SDK collects device and advertising identifiers, an identifier of its own, information about the ads you see, your approximate location, and crash and performance data, to show and measure ads. Unless you allow tracking, the app tells Pangle not to personalize ads for you, and your choices in Google's consent form apply to Pangle too. See Pangle's Privacy Policy at www.pangleglobal.com/privacy.

Some ads may come from Mintegral, an ad network that competes through Google AdMob for the ads in the app; its SDK is part of the app. Mintegral's SDK receives information about your device, each ad request and the ads you see, to show and measure ads. Unless you allow tracking, the app tells Mintegral not to personalize ads for you or share your information with its partners, and your choices in Google's consent form apply to Mintegral too. See Mintegral's Privacy Policy at www.mintegral.com/en/privacy.

Some ads may come from Unity Ads, an ad network that competes through Google AdMob for the ads in the app; its SDK is part of the app. Unity's SDK receives information about your device, each ad request and the ads you see, to show and measure ads. Unless you allow tracking, the app opts you out of Unity's data collection and personalized ads, and your choices in Google's consent form apply to Unity Ads too. See Unity's privacy policy for app users at unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

The app also includes Meta's SDK (the Facebook SDK), which measures how well our own ads for the app on Facebook and Instagram work. From the moment the app opens, the SDK sends Meta app events, such as the app being installed or opened and purchases made in the app, with information about your device (its model, system version, language and time zone), an identifier of Meta's own and crash data. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is included only if you allow tracking when Apple asks. Meta uses this information to measure and show ads, and for analytics. See Meta's Privacy Policy at www.facebook.com/privacy/policy.

由應用程式 4.7 版起，訪客及免費帳戶會看到廣告。廣告由 Google AdMob 透過 Google Mobile Ads SDK 提供。所有付費計劃（Plus、Pro、Max 5× 及 Max 20×）均不會顯示廣告。

Google Mobile Ads SDK 會收集你的大約位置（根據你的 IP 位址推算）、裝置識別碼、你看過及點按過的廣告的資料、你使用應用程式的方式，以及診斷、效能及當機資料。Google 會把這些資料用於顯示廣告、衡量廣告成效及分析。

只有在 Apple 詢問而你允許追蹤時，才會讀取廣告識別碼（IDFA）。已登入的免費帳戶會在完成第一份測驗後被詢問一次，詢問前應用程式會先顯示一段只有一個按鈕的簡短說明。你可隨時在裝置的「設定」▸「私隱與保安」▸「追蹤」更改你的答覆。

在歐洲經濟區、英國及瑞士，應用程式會在要求任何廣告之前先顯示 Google 的同意表格。你其後可在應用程式的「設定」▸「私隱選項」更改你的選擇。

當你觀看廣告以賺取代幣時，應用程式會把你帳戶的內部識別碼（絕不包括你的姓名或電話號碼）及一個隨機編碼傳送給 Google，讓我們的伺服器可以向 Google 確認獎勵。每次發放的代幣均會被記錄。

如欲了解 Google 如何使用來自採用其服務的應用程式的資料，請參閱 policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites，以及 Google 私隱政策 policies.google.com/privacy。

部分廣告可能來自 Pangle。Pangle 是透過 Google AdMob 競逐應用程式內廣告的廣告網絡，其 SDK 是應用程式的一部分。Pangle 的 SDK 會收集裝置及廣告識別碼、其本身的識別碼、你看過的廣告的資料、你的大約位置，以及當機及效能資料，用於顯示廣告及衡量廣告成效。除非你允許追蹤，否則應用程式會通知 Pangle 不要為你提供個人化廣告；你在 Google 同意表格中的選擇亦同樣適用於 Pangle。請參閱 Pangle 私隱政策 www.pangleglobal.com/privacy。

部分廣告可能來自 Mintegral。Mintegral 是透過 Google AdMob 競逐應用程式內廣告的廣告網絡，其 SDK 是應用程式的一部分。Mintegral 的 SDK 會接收有關你的裝置、每個廣告要求及你看過的廣告的資料，用於顯示廣告及衡量廣告成效。除非你允許追蹤，否則應用程式會通知 Mintegral 不要為你提供個人化廣告，亦不要與其合作夥伴分享你的資料；你在 Google 同意表格中的選擇亦同樣適用於 Mintegral。請參閱 Mintegral 私隱政策 www.mintegral.com/en/privacy。

部分廣告可能來自 Unity Ads。Unity Ads 是透過 Google AdMob 競逐應用程式內廣告的廣告網絡，其 SDK 是應用程式的一部分。Unity 的 SDK 會接收有關你的裝置、每個廣告要求及你看過的廣告的資料，用於顯示廣告及衡量廣告成效。除非你允許追蹤，否則應用程式會替你拒絕 Unity 的資料收集及個人化廣告；你在 Google 同意表格中的選擇亦同樣適用於 Unity Ads。請參閱 Unity 的應用程式用戶私隱政策 unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy。

應用程式亦包含 Meta 的 SDK（Facebook SDK），用於衡量我們在 Facebook 及 Instagram 上為應用程式刊登的廣告的成效。應用程式一經開啟，該 SDK 便會向 Meta 傳送應用程式事件（例如安裝及開啟應用程式，以及在應用程式內購買），連同你裝置的資料（型號、系統版本、語言及時區）、Meta 本身的識別碼及當機資料。只有在 Apple 詢問而你允許追蹤時，才會包括廣告識別碼（IDFA）。Meta 會把這些資料用於衡量廣告成效、顯示廣告及分析。請參閱 Meta 私隱政策 www.facebook.com/privacy/policy。
