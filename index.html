How does the JUPAS Calculator work out my score?聯招計分器點樣計我嘅分數？
Every programme is scored with its own published formula — its subject weightings, its best-five or best-six rule, its 5** conversion and any bonus — so the same results give different scores for different programmes, and each step is shown. Where a university's rules can be read two ways, you see both scores. So far the app shows your score for 363 programmes, each one checked against the university's own document; the others show their admission statistics only. Figures come from JUPAS and each university's admissions-score page, and they are for reference only — universities do not admit on HKDSE results alone, and past scores do not predict later years. 每個課程都用佢自己公布嘅計分方法計 — 科目比重、揀最佳五科定六科、5**點樣換算、有冇加分 — 所以同一份成績，唔同課程會計出唔同分數，而且每一步都列出嚟。院校規則有兩種讀法嘅，兩個分數都會顯示。App暫時為363個課程計分，每個都已經同院校原文核對；其餘課程只顯示入學數據。資料來自JUPAS同各院校嘅入學分數網頁，僅供參考 — 院校唔會只按文憑試成績取錄，過往分數亦唔代表日後結果。

Can I be logged in on multiple devices?可唔可以幾部機同時login？
Your account works on both iPhone and iPad, but for security reasons only one device can be logged in at a time — signing in on your phone signs the iPad out, and vice versa. If you find yourself logged out, check whether another device is using your account. 帳戶iPhone同iPad都用得，但基於安全理由，同一時間只可以喺一部裝置login — 喺電話登入，iPad就會被登出，反之亦然。如果發現自己被登出，請檢查係咪有另一部裝置正在使用你嘅帳戶。
