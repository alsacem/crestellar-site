How does the Daily Plan choose my questions?每日計劃點樣幫我揀題？
It starts from a short level check — 12 questions, easy first. Each day's quiz is then built for you: a warm-up from your strongest topic, main practice on the two topics you most need (each at its own level), questions you missed before to try again, and in a 10- or 15-question quiz a slightly harder bonus question to finish. Questions stay within your form and the topics you have ticked as learned, and a question you have done is not asked again for 30 days, except the ones you try again. Long Questions you mark yourself (“I got it” or “Not yet”) count too, at half the weight of a quiz answer. Your level estimate moves with your answers, so the quiz moves with you — it is an estimate from your practice, not an exam result. Choose 5, 10 or 15 questions a day; there is no time limit. 先做一個簡短嘅程度檢測 — 12題，由淺入深。之後每日嘅測驗都為你度身揀題：先用你最擅長嘅課題熱身，再集中練你最需要加強嘅兩個課題（每個課題按自己嘅程度出題），跟住係之前答錯嘅題目再試一次；10題或15題嘅測驗，最後仲有一條略難嘅挑戰題。題目唔會超出你嘅年級同你剔咗已學嘅課題，做過嘅題目30日內唔會再出（再試一次嗰啲除外）。你喺長題目嘅自評（「做對了」或「還未做到」）都會計入，份量係測驗答案嘅一半。你嘅程度估計會跟住你嘅答案調整，所以測驗會跟你一齊進步 — 程度係根據你嘅練習估計出嚟，唔係考試成績。每日揀5、10或15題，冇時間限制。
How does the JUPAS Calculator work out my score?聯招計分器點樣計我嘅分數？
Every programme is scored with its own published formula — its subject weightings, its best-five or best-six rule, its 5** conversion and any bonus — so the same results give different scores for different programmes, and each step is shown. Where a university's rules can be read two ways, you see both scores. So far the app shows your score for 365 programmes, each one checked against the university's own document; the others show their admission statistics only. Figures come from JUPAS and each university's admissions-score page, and they are for reference only — universities do not admit on HKDSE results alone, and past scores do not predict later years. 每個課程都用佢自己公布嘅計分方法計 — 科目比重、揀最佳五科定六科、5**點樣換算、有冇加分 — 所以同一份成績，唔同課程會計出唔同分數，而且每一步都列出嚟。院校規則有兩種讀法嘅，兩個分數都會顯示。App暫時為365個課程計分，每個都已經同院校原文核對；其餘課程只顯示入學數據。資料來自JUPAS同各院校嘅入學分數網頁，僅供參考 — 院校唔會只按文憑試成績取錄，過往分數亦唔代表日後結果。
How close is the Exam Calculator to a real calculator?考試計算機同真計算機有幾似？

Your two calculators — their memories, answers and programs — are kept on the device, the way a real calculator keeps them: they stay when you sign out, and anyone who signs in on the same iPad or iPhone finds the same calculators. In the exam you still use your own calculator. 佢跟足原裝說明書，再用真機逐樣對過：同樣嘅按鍵排列同兩行顯示、六個模式、23條公式同40個常數，筆記同解法教嘅按鍵次序可以照住練。佢係我哋自己做嘅模擬，唔係計算機廠商嘅軟件。兩部計算機（記憶、答案同程式）都儲喺部機度，好似真計算機咁：登出之後都仲喺度，喺同一部iPad或iPhone登入嘅人都會見到同一批計算機。考試嗰陣就要用返你自己部計算機。
